Where are you located?

Suite 155 at 16101 Ventura Boulevard, in Encino — the quiet end of the Valley's best-known street, minutes from Sherman Oaks, Studio City and Tarzana. Parking is easy, and you'll never be rushed through the door.

Which treatments do you offer?

Medical-grade facials — Hydrafacial, dermaplaning, oxygen and cold-infusion protocols — alongside HIFU non-surgical lifting, plasma pen tightening, Japanese and Korean head spa rituals, body and sculpting therapies, physician-led stem cell therapy, and a private Afternoon Tea lounge. Aesthetics and wellness, under one roof.

How is pricing handled?

Every price is published on our treatment menu, from body rituals to advanced lifting. You will never be quoted a figure you haven't already seen, and nothing is added at checkout.

How does a first visit begin?

With a conversation, not a procedure. Your first visit opens with a complimentary assessment of your skin, your goals and the pace that suits your life — after which you receive a considered plan. You are welcome to begin the same day, or to take the plan home and think on it.

Are you open on weekends?

Every day of the week, ten until seven — Saturday and Sunday included. Most of Encino closes when your schedule finally opens; we don't.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Allow an additional fifteen minutes. You will be received privately, assessed unhurriedly, and given a clear treatment plan with pricing before anything begins. One guest, one protocol, no waiting-room atmosphere.

How do I reserve an appointment?

Online in under a minute, or by telephone at (818) 437-3204. Same-week appointments are usually available, weekends included — though evenings tend to reserve first.

How should I prepare?

Arrive with clean skin where possible, and set aside retinoids and strong exfoliants for the forty-eight hours beforehand. Please share your full medical history at consultation — certain treatments are not advised during pregnancy, while nursing, or alongside particular conditions.
