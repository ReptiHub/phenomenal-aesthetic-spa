Enquiries

Answered before you ask

Where are you located?

Suite 155 at 16101 Ventura Boulevard, in Encino — the quiet end of the Valley's best-known street, minutes from Sherman Oaks, Studio City and Tarzana. Parking is covered and validated, and you will not be rushed through the door.

Which treatments do you offer?

Medical-grade facials — Hydrafacial, dermaplaning, oxygen and cold-infusion protocols — alongside HIFU non-surgical lifting, plasma pen tightening, Japanese and Korean head spa rituals, body and sculpting therapies, physician-led stem cell therapy, and a private Afternoon Tea lounge. Aesthetics and wellness, under one roof.

How does a first visit begin?

With a conversation, not a procedure. Your first visit opens with a complimentary assessment of your skin, your goals and the pace that suits your life — after which you receive a considered plan. You are welcome to begin the same day, or to take the plan home and think on it.

Are you open on weekends?

Every day of the week, ten until seven — Saturday and Sunday included. Most of Encino closes when your schedule finally opens; we don't.
