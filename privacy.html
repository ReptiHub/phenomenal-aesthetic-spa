Your Privacy

Held in confidence.

Discretion is part of the service. What we hold, why we hold it, and the control that remains yours throughout.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Phenomenal Aesthetic Medical & Wellness Spa ("Phenomenal," "we," "us") operates this website and provides medical aesthetic and wellness services at 16101 Ventura Blvd, Suite 155, Encino, CA 91436. This policy describes how we handle information collected through this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — your name, phone number, email address and any details you share when you call us, request a consultation, or book an appointment.
  • Booking information — appointments are processed through GlossGenius, our third-party booking platform. Information you enter there is governed by GlossGenius's own privacy policy.
  • Technical information — standard web-server data such as browser type, device type, referring page and pages viewed. This site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Health information — any medical history you disclose during a consultation is collected in person or through our booking platform, not through this website, and is treated as confidential.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and provide the services you request
  • To respond to your enquiries and consultation requests
  • To determine treatment suitability and maintain your care record
  • To meet legal, medical and record-keeping obligations

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not share your health information with advertisers or data brokers. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as our booking platform), and only as needed to deliver your care.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect your information, and limit access to staff who need it to provide your care. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, and request details of how it has been shared. Certain medical records are subject to retention requirements under California and federal law and may not be deletable on request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, call (818) 437-3204 or email phenomenalskincare24@gmail.com.

Third-party links

This site links to external services, including our GlossGenius booking page, Google Maps and Instagram. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revision date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
